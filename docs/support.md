# Support

## Email us

**[support@publora.com](mailto:support@publora.com)** is the fastest way to reach a person. We answer within one business day.

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. If a post failed, include the post group ID from the dashboard: it lets us find the exact request in our logs.

## Account, billing and connected channels

Everything to do with your plan, your API keys and the social accounts you have connected lives in the dashboard at **[app.publora.com](https://app.publora.com)**. API keys are under [Settings → API keys](https://app.publora.com/dashboard/api).

If you cannot sign in, write to [support@publora.com](mailto:support@publora.com) from the address you registered with.

## Reporting a bug

Send it to [support@publora.com](mailto:support@publora.com) with the endpoint you called, the time in UTC, and the response you received. For anything involving a scheduled post, the post group ID is the single most useful detail.

## Security

Found something that looks like a vulnerability? Write to [support@publora.com](mailto:support@publora.com) with `security` in the subject line and please do not disclose it publicly until we have replied.
